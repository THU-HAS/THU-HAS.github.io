THU Hardware Architecture & Security

Welcome to THU-HAS Lab. Our research advances efficient, secure computing from processor microarchitectures to chips, chiplets, and interconnects.

Research Focus 1
Endogenous Efficiency and Security in Computing Microarchitectures
  • By systematically exploring performance design spaces and attack surfaces from prefetchers to memory-access units, we proposed the first timer-free generic cache-attack primitive. This research earned public acknowledgment from SiFive and directly informed China's open-source XiangShan processor.
  • The related prefetcher-attack work received the Best Paper Award at AsianHOST 2025.
Research Focus 2
Efficiency and Security in Novel Computing Chips and Chiplets
  • We constructed a high-precision reverse-engineering and attack framework for cross-chiplet secure domains.
  • We performed the first systematic evaluation exposing the fragility of weight-obfuscation defenses in Computing-in-Memory chips, demonstrating a key-parsing success rate exceeding 90%. This was the Department of Electronic Engineering's first lead-institution paper at a top information-security conference.
Research Focus 3
Efficient and Secure Computing Interconnects
  • We systematically characterized and quantified performance interference in CXL memory, restoring main-memory bandwidth to 99%.
  • We designed a CXL-secure key-value-store framework for multi-host sharing, delivering 3.45 times the throughput of advanced Redis implementations while preserving isolation across shared-memory resources.

Shuwen Deng

Associate Professor

Tsinghua University

Dr. Shuwen Deng is currently a tenure-track associate professor in the Department of Electronic Engineering at Tsinghua University. She received her bachelor’s degree in engineering from the Department of Micro-Nano Electronics at Shanghai Jiao Tong University and her PhD in engineering from the Department of Electrical Engineering at Yale University. Her main research focuses on building efficient and secure computing infrastructure in the fields of computer architecture and hardware architecture. She has published multiple papers as the first author and corresponding author at top academic conferences such as ISCA, ASPLOS, MICRO, HPCA, CCS, and NDSS. She was awarded the MICRO 2026 Distinguished Paper Honorable Mention, the 2026 First-Prize Teaching Achievement Award of the Chinese Institute of Electronics (2), the 2025 AsianHOST Best Paper Award, the 2023 IEEE HOST Best PhD Dissertation Award, the 2021 IEEE Hardware and Embedded Security Top Picks Award, the 2020 Google PhD Fellowship in Privacy and Security, and the Dickerman Fellowship.
